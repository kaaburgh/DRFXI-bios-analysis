## observations
- the firmware components on the re-entry path are unchanged by 1.17: the variable-protection driver is byte-identical, the CBS/AOD drivers and APCB service differ only by PCD renumbering, the question tables are identical
- every DDR5 primary/secondary timing and turnaround with a menu field is settable in both CBS and AOD with identical ranges (AOD adds step 2 for Tcl, step 6 for Twr);
  tCWL, tREFI, tCKE, tSTAG, tMOD, GDM, Cmd2T and Nitro have no menu field and are AGESA-derived on both builds;
  the stock Setup UI hides the DRAM tuning questions via AMITSE access metadata (findings/hii-ifr.md, clean-hii-unlock.md); the questions themselves are registered in HII identically on 1.15 and 1.17
- a stored-variable edit that never went through a Setup save shows up as DIVERGES (setup value would be pushed, SPI APCB lacks the token)
- --compare reduces 'did the re-entry reproduce the 1.15 configuration' to a binary result; build IDs and APOB state are informational only
