Focused IFR evidence: Above 4G Decoding visibility change
========================================================

Source firmware hashes:
  1.12  246e7f54a12a8c7279ef68433602264b8ecbabcde828c9c9008f571c270845b9
  1.15  3afaba6d916b3897d1d98bd5913008b38e89b6c51b2c6963c7109793541d8d88
  1.17  99f3bab5ea491202bf365c23e6416f9d1e8a649ad00f90c1f3e21f1a61e35c51

Source HII module: Setup
Underlying setting: VarStoreId 0xF101, VarOffset 0x9D

1.12 has a normally exposed copy:

    OneOf Prompt: "Above 4G Decoding",
          QuestionId: 0x57,
          VarStoreId: 0xF101,
          VarOffset: 0x9D
        OneOfOption "Disabled" = 0
        OneOfOption "Enabled"  = 1
        Default = 1
    End

1.12 also has a second copy buried under permanent suppression:

    SuppressIf
        Uint64 Value: 0x1
        ...
        OneOf Prompt: "Above 4G Decoding",
              QuestionId: 0xEC,
              VarStoreId: 0xF101,
              VarOffset: 0x9D
        ...
    End

In 1.15 the normally exposed copy is gone. The suppressed copy remains, now with shifted QuestionId 0xEB but the same VarStore/offset 0x9D. 1.17 retains the 1.15 behavior.

Conclusion:
  This is direct HII evidence for the 1.14 changelog entry `Hide Above 4G Decoding`. The setting itself remains represented; its exposed UI entry was removed/hidden.
